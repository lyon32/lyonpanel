# Prompt de référence du logo LyonPanel

## Prompt principal
Professional minimalist logo for "LyonPanel", featuring a bold royal blue capital letter "P" whose bowl is dynamically traversed by a sleek upward-pointing golden ochre growth arrow. Clean flat vector design, modern geometric sans-serif wordmark "LyonPanel" to the right in split royal blue and golden ochre, with smaller subtitle text "African Market Panel" underneath in black. Clean solid white background, flat graphic design, corporate tech style, high resolution, vector art, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects.

## Negative prompt (Stable Diffusion et modèles compatibles)
gradients, 3D, shadows, realistic photo, complex background, blurry, low resolution, extra text, watermark, misspelled text, distorted letters

- Bleu roi : #1D4ED8
